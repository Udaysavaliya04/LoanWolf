# LoanWolf

Track shifting rates, simulate extra payments, and escape loan debt faster.

Try now: https://loanwolf-platform.onrender.com

LoanWolf is a loan payoff intelligence platform that helps borrowers understand what their bank rarely shows them clearly: the full month-by-month story of their loan.

Most banks give borrowers a basic summary: principal, EMI, interest rate, tenure, and outstanding balance. But they usually do not provide a clean interactive amortization schedule online, especially one that shows how every EMI is split between interest and principal, how rate changes affect the future, and how much faster prepayments can close the loan. In many cases, borrowers have to repeatedly ask, call, email, visit branches, or practically beg the bank just to get a detailed repayment schedule.

LoanWolf gives that power back to the borrower.

## The Problem

Loans are designed to look simple from the outside, but the real cost hides inside the amortization schedule.

In the early years of a long loan, a large part of every EMI goes toward interest instead of principal. When rates increase, banks may quietly extend the tenure instead of making the impact obvious. When borrowers make prepayments, they often cannot clearly see how much interest they saved or how many months they removed from the loan.

Without a transparent schedule, borrowers are forced to guess.

LoanWolf turns those guesses into numbers.

## What LoanWolf Does

- Creates detailed loan profiles for home, personal, auto, education, business, and other loans.
- Generates a full month-by-month amortization schedule.
- Shows opening balance, EMI, interest, principal, prepayment, and closing balance for every period.
- Tracks extra principal payments and shows their real effect.
- Tracks interest rate changes for floating-rate loans.
- Compares the original loan path against the updated path after prepayments or rate changes.
- Calculates interest saved, EMIs saved, total paid, remaining balance, and payoff date.
- Visualizes remaining principal over time.
- Breaks down yearly interest vs principal payments.
- Supports multiple currencies.
- Exports professional amortization statements as PDF files.
- Provides a profile dashboard with debt overview, payoff timeline, and prepayment milestones.
- Includes a loan education section to explain amortization, prepayments, rate hikes, and debt strategy.
- Includes WolfAI, an AI debt strategist that can answer questions about the user's loan portfolio.
- Supports feedback so users can share issues, suggestions, or product improvements.

## Key Features

### Amortization Schedule

LoanWolf shows the full repayment timeline instead of hiding it behind a summary number. Borrowers can see exactly how much of each EMI goes to interest, how much reduces principal, and how the balance changes month by month.

### Extra Payment Simulation

Users can add prepayments and instantly see how they affect the loan. Since prepayments directly reduce principal, LoanWolf shows the resulting interest savings and how much earlier the loan can be closed.

### Floating Rate Tracking

For loans where interest rates change over time, users can add rate-change events. LoanWolf recalculates the schedule from that point onward so borrowers can understand how a small rate increase can stretch the loan.

### What-If Planning

Borrowers can test scenarios before committing money: a lump-sum prepayment, a future rate hike, or a different repayment plan. This helps them decide whether to increase EMI, make a prepayment, or prioritize another loan.

### WolfAI Debt Advisor

WolfAI can analyze the user's loans and answer practical questions like which loan to repay first, how much extra to pay monthly, how rate changes affect the payoff date, and how to reduce interest using an avalanche-style strategy.

### PDF Export

LoanWolf can generate a polished amortization statement that users can keep for their records, compare with bank documents, or use while planning repayments.

## How It Helps Repay Loans Faster

LoanWolf helps borrowers repay loans faster by showing where every extra rupee, dollar, euro, or pound can create the biggest impact.

When a borrower makes an early prepayment, the principal falls sooner. A lower principal means future interest is calculated on a smaller balance. That can reduce total interest, shorten the tenure, and bring the payoff date closer.

LoanWolf makes this visible before the borrower acts. Instead of wondering whether an extra payment is worth it, users can simulate it, compare the result, and choose a smarter repayment strategy.
